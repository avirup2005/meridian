FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is this just a ChatGPT wrapper?

Absolutely not. We handle our own semantic chunking, embedding generation using PyTorch and Sentence Transformers, and vector indexing using FAISS. We only use Gemini for the final synthesis layer, strictly constrained by our RAG pipeline.

How do you handle massive 300+ page PDFs?

We use Layout-Aware parsing to break the document down logically (preserving headers, tables, and paragraphs), embed the chunks, and retrieve only the top-K relevant passages per query. We don't shove the whole PDF into the context window.

What if the AI hallucinates a financial number?

Our prompt engineering explicitly forbids the AI from generating numbers not found in the retrieved context. Furthermore, every extracted metric is linked back to a specific page and paragraph in the source document. Receipts required.

Why the crazy design?

Because Neo-Brutalism enforces high contrast, clarity, and focus. Also, it looks insanely cool and immediately stands out from the sea of boring corporate SaaS platforms. We are hacking finance.
